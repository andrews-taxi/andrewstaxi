October is one of the best months to explore Lebanon. The heat has eased, the light is softer, and the coast, mountains and ancient sites are all comfortable to visit. You also don't need a rental car or a rigid group tour. A **Lebanon private driver** lets you set your own pace, stop where you like and skip the parking and navigation.

Here are five day trips from Beirut that suit autumn. Each one can be booked with Andrews Taxi at a fixed price, 24/7.

## 1. Byblos: ancient harbour and old souk

Byblos is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. It is an easy first trip. Walk the Crusader castle, the Roman ruins and the old stone souk, then have lunch by the harbour.

A **taxi to Byblos** from Beirut is a straightforward ride along the coast. If you are arriving in Lebanon, you can go straight from [Beirut Airport (BEY)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) to [Byblos (Jbeil)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) and skip the city.

## 2. Batroun: sea, lemonade and old-town streets

Continue north from Byblos to [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). The old town is good for walking, and the seafront has cafés and seafood. Autumn is a good time to visit because the sea breeze is pleasant without summer crowds. Byblos and Batroun combine well into one day with a single driver who waits for you.

## 3. Mount Lebanon: Faqra, Beit Mery and Bikfaya

You can see a lot of Mount Lebanon in one day. Faqra has Roman and Phoenician temple ruins, and the villages around it have pine forests and views over the valleys. For a longer mountain day, add [Faraya / Kfardebian](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). Mountain roads are winding, so an experienced local driver is a real advantage.

## 4. Saida (Sidon): sea castle and old market

Head south to [Saida (Sidon)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). It has a winding old marketplace, a castle on the sea and a strong local heritage. If you want to go further, [Tyre (Sour)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) is a natural next stop. Both are good in October, when the weather suits walking tours.

## 5. Baalbek and Zahle: Roman temples and the Bekaa Valley

For a full day out, go east to [Baalbek](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) to see the Roman temples. On the way, stop at [Zahle](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) for a long Lebanese lunch. This is a longer day, so a comfortable car and a driver who knows the road make a big difference.
