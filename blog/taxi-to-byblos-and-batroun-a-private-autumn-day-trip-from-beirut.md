# Taxi to Byblos and Batroun: A Private Autumn Day Trip from Beirut

October and November are some of the best months to explore the Lebanese coast. The heat of summer has passed, the seafront is calmer, and the old stone streets are easier to enjoy. A day trip north from Beirut is one of the simplest ways to see a lot in a short time.

With a **Lebanon private driver**, you don't have to deal with parking, bargaining or changing buses. You set the schedule, and the car waits for you.

## Why Go to Byblos and Batroun in Autumn?

- **Fewer crowds.** Weekends in peak summer can be busy. Autumn gives you more space at the sites and in the cafés.
- **Comfortable weather.** Walking through ruins and souks is more pleasant when it isn't scorching.
- **Easy to combine.** Byblos and Batroun sit on the same coastal road, so you can see both in one day.

## Stop 1: Byblos (Jbeil)

Byblos is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. A **taxi to Byblos** takes you straight to the old town, where you can:

- Walk through the archaeological site and the Crusader castle.
- Browse the souk for handmade crafts.
- Visit Saint John-Mark Cathedral and its gardens.
- Have a seafood lunch by the old port.

## Stop 2: Batroun

Further north, Batroun is known for its old walls, its beaches and its relaxed pace. Many visitors come for:

- The old town and the seafront.
- Local craft beer and wineries in the nearby hills.
- The Nabu Museum.
- Coastal viewpoints and sunset walks.

A **taxi to Batroun** from Beirut or Byblos lets you enjoy a drink or a glass of wine without worrying about the drive back. You can check your route to [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) before you go.
