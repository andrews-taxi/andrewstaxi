**Practical tips for a taxi to AUBMC hospital:**

## Safe Taxi for Elderly Parents in Beirut: What Makes the Difference

Choosing a **safe taxi for elderly parents in Beirut** is about more than the destination. Here is what we focus on:

### 1. A Calm, Unhurried Driver
Our drivers have 30+ years of local experience. They drive smoothly, brake gently, and avoid sudden maneuvers. They also know that an older passenger may need extra time getting in and out.

### 2. Help Getting In and Out
Drivers will open the door and give a steady arm if you want it. They'll take your bag or walking stick, and fold a standard foldable wheelchair or walker into the trunk. Tell us in advance if the passenger uses one, so we can send the right vehicle.

### 3. The Right Vehicle for the Passenger
- **[Sedan](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator):** Good for one or two passengers who can sit comfortably in a standard seat.
- **[SUV](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator):** Higher seating makes getting in and out easier for many seniors. There is also more room for a walker or folded wheelchair.
- **[Minivan / Van](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator):** Best when several relatives travel with the patient, or when there is a lot of equipment.

### 4. Clean, Air-Conditioned Cars
Cool in summer, comfortable in winter, and always clean. It matters more when a patient is tired or unwell.

### 5. Fixed, Transparent Pricing
You get the price before you travel, in USD or LBP. There are no meter surprises and no haggling at the hospital entrance. You also don't pay extra because you are in a hurry.
