# November Day Trips from Beirut: Byblos, Batroun and Faraya with a Private Driver

November is a good month to explore Lebanon. The summer crowds have gone and the air is cooler. The coast is still pleasant for walking, and the mountains are starting to feel like winter. Spring and autumn are the best seasons for Batroun, and the same goes for most of the coast.

## Day Trip 1: Byblos (Jbeil), a taxi to one of the world's oldest cities

A **taxi to Byblos** is the easiest first trip for visitors. Byblos is often cited as one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. It combines ancient ruins, a castle, a picturesque harbor and plenty of places for a seaside coffee, all within walking distance.

**Suggested plan:**
1. Leave Beirut in the morning and head north to [Byblos (Jbeil)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator).
2. Visit the castle and archaeological site, then walk through the old souks.
3. Have lunch near the harbor.
4. Your driver can wait for you or return at an agreed time.

## Day Trip 2: Batroun, coastal charm and sunset

Further north, [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) is a good mix of old-town character and modern café and nightlife culture. In autumn the weather is pleasant and it is less crowded. Walk the old streets, see the harbor, and browse the shops in the old souks.

Combine Byblos and Batroun in one day and stay for dinner. A private driver means no one has to stay sober behind the wheel, so you can enjoy the evening and ride back to Beirut comfortably.

## Day Trip 3: Faraya and Kfardebian, the first taste of winter

Head up into the mountains to [Faraya / Kfardebian](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). The slopes get snow in winter, and in the shoulder season the area is good for crisp air, mountain views and cozy lunches. Mountain roads can change quickly with the weather, so a driver who knows them is a real advantage. Check the forecast before you go and tell us if you plan a late return.

## Practical tips for autumn travel in Lebanon

- **Check the weather.** Autumn rain can affect mountain roads. Tell your driver where you are headed so they can plan the route.
- **Carry some cash.** Not every small shop or site takes cards.
- **Get travel insurance that covers Lebanon.** Some standard policies exclude it, so confirm before you travel.
- **Start early.** Daylight is shorter in November, so an early departure gives you more time at each stop.
