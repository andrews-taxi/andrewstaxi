## Why Event Transport in Lebanon Needs Planning

Lebanon's best venues are rarely close to a main road, and they are almost never close to a reliable late-night taxi rank.

- **Beach clubs and party beaches** sit on stretches of coast with no public transport. This is true of Batroun, Thoum and the coast south of Beirut around Damour.
- **Mountain wedding venues** in the Chouf, Broummana, Faitroun, Baissour and the hills above Metn and Keserwan are reached by narrow, winding roads. These are dark and sometimes foggy at night.
- **Parking is limited.** Large events can leave guests parked along a verge, blocked in, or walking a long way to their cars.
- **Late-night availability is unpredictable.** Apps and street taxis thin out after midnight, and prices can jump without warning.
- **Nobody should drive after drinking.** A pre-booked driver removes the problem, and the whole group gets home safely.

A pre-arranged group ride solves all of this. You know the price, the vehicle and the pickup time before you leave home.

## Beach Club Taxi Batroun: Door-to-Door Without the Stress

Damour sits on the coast road south of Beirut, on the way to Saida and the Chouf foothills. It is much closer to the capital than Batroun, so it suits quick escapes, daytime beach sessions that stretch into the evening, and seaside events along the southern coastline.
