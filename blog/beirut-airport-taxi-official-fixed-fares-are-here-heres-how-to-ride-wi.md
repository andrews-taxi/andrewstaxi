# Beirut Airport Taxi: Official Fixed Fares Are Here

For years, the first thing many travelers did after landing in Lebanon was negotiate. You collected your bags, walked out of arrivals, and then argued about the price of a ride. Recent reports say the Public Works Ministry has stepped in with an official fare schedule for taxis leaving Beirut airport. Passengers can reportedly check it by scanning a QR code on screens at the airport and on leaflets handed out at passport control.

We think this is good news for everyone. At Andrews Taxi, fixed pricing has always been our standard. Here is what the change means for you, and how to plan a smooth arrival.

## Why Fixed Fares Matter

- **No surprises.** You know the cost before the car moves.
- **No haggling after a long flight.** You arrive tired, and the ride should be simple.
- **Easier budgeting.** This helps tourists, business travelers and returning families alike.

Check the official schedule on your phone when you land. Then compare it with a quote you booked in advance. A booked ride gives you the price, the driver and the pickup point confirmed before you leave home.
