## 8 Tips for a Calm Ride with Your Dog or Cat

Our drivers have carried many nervous passengers. These habits work best.

1. **Tell us the species, size and temperament when you book.** "Large, friendly Labrador" and "anxious cat in a hard carrier" lead to different vehicles.
2. **Use a secure carrier or crate for cats and small dogs.** It keeps your pet safe if the driver brakes hard on the Autostrade or in Beirut traffic.
3. **Bring a familiar blanket or toy.** The smell of home calms most animals.
4. **Skip the big meal beforehand.** A light stomach helps with car sickness. Ask your vet if your pet needs anything else.
5. **Pack a towel and waste bags.** A towel under the carrier protects the seat and soaks up accidents.
6. **Keep the leash on until you're in the car.** Doors opening near Corniche traffic are the riskiest moment, so keep a firm grip.
7. **Sit beside your pet, not in front.** Your presence reassures them.
8. **Ask for a quiet start.** We'll keep the radio low, the A/C comfortable and the driving smooth. Cats and anxious dogs notice the difference.
