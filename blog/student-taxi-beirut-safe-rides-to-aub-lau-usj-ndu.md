## Why Greater Beirut Students Struggle With Campus Commutes

Most students at Beirut's major universities live at home and commute. Public transport is limited, and the campuses sit in some of the busiest parts of the city. The usual problems are:

- **Morning congestion around Hamra, Koraytem and Ras Beirut.** Around 8 a.m. it can take a long time just to reach the gate or find parking.
- **Unreliable timing.** A late ride can mean a missed quiz, a missed lab or a missed exam.
- **Parking costs and stress.** Daily fuel, parking fees and car wear add up quickly.
- **Late-evening safety.** Study groups, labs, clubs and exam season push many students out after dark.
- **Long-distance campuses.** Students coming from the mountains, Metn, Keserwan or the south face long drives on the Autostrade.

A pre-booked student taxi fixes most of this. You get a fixed pickup time, a vetted driver, a known price and no parking hunt.
