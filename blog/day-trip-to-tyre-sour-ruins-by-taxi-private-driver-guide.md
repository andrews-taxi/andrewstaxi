## What to See on a Tyre Sour Ruins Day Trip

Tyre has two main archaeological areas, and both are UNESCO-listed. Visit them at different times of day.

### 1. Al-Bass Archaeological Site: Hippodrome, Necropolis and Roman Road
Al-Bass is the highlight for most history lovers.
- **The Roman Hippodrome** was a chariot-racing stadium, among the largest of its kind in the Roman world. Many visitors have it nearly to themselves.
- **The necropolis** has sarcophagi and tombs from the Roman and Byzantine periods.
- **The colonnaded Roman road and the monumental arch** show how the ancient city was laid out.

**Tip:** Go in the morning for cooler temperatures and better light. Bring a hat, water and comfortable shoes.

### 2. Al-Mina Archaeological Site: Ancient Harbour City
Al-Mina is closer to the old port and has columns, streets and the remains of Roman-era buildings near the sea. Late afternoon is the best time, when the low light warms the stone and the Mediterranean behind it.

### 3. Tyre's Fishing Port and Old Town
Walk the harbour, watch the boats and browse the small shops. Stop at a local pastry shop for something sweet before heading back.

A **Sidon sea castle taxi tour** fits naturally into the road to or from Tyre. Saida (Sidon) is a 30–45 minute drive from Beirut.
