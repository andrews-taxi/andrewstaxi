# Beirut Airport Taxi to Byblos and Batroun: A Fixed-Price Autumn Day Trip

October is a good month to see the Lebanese coast. The summer crowds have thinned and the heat has eased. The old stone streets of Byblos and the seafront in Batroun are pleasant to walk. A **Lebanon private driver** makes the trip easy, because you don't have to deal with minibus schedules, parking or fare negotiations.

## Stop 1: Byblos (Jbeil), About 45 Minutes From the Airport

Byblos is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, and a **taxi to Byblos** is the easiest way to reach it. Here is what to see:

- **The Crusader castle and archaeological site.** The ruins span thousands of years of history.
- **The old souk.** It sells handmade crafts, local sweets and souvenirs.
- **The old harbor.** It is a good place for a seafood lunch or an afternoon coffee.

You can go straight from the airport to [Byblos (Jbeil)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) and leave your luggage in the car with your driver while you explore. You can also drop your bags at your hotel first.

## Stop 2: Batroun, a Short Drive North

From Byblos, Batroun is a short drive up the coast. It is a Phoenician-era town that now has a lively food and nightlife scene. In autumn you can:

- Walk the old streets, the seafront and the Phoenician sea wall.
- Visit the **Nabu Museum** near the coast.
- Try the local craft beer and visit wineries in the Batroun hills. A wine festival is often held here in autumn.
- Have dinner at a seafront restaurant and stay for the evening.

Pick up a ride to [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) directly from Byblos, or book a round trip so your driver waits for you.
