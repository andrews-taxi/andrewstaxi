# Beirut Nightlife by Taxi: Gemmayze, Hamra and Late-Night Food, Without the Parking Stress

Beirut's night starts late and finishes later. Rooftop lounges, wine bars, cocktail spots and late-night kitchens all run on their own clock. Getting to them and back is the hard part: parking is scarce, streets are busy, and nobody should drive after a long dinner.

At Andrews Taxi, we cover Beirut's nightlife areas around the clock, at a **fixed price agreed before you ride**.

## Where Beirut's Nightlife Is Right Now

Most of the city's evening energy sits in a few areas:

- **Gemmayze and Mar Mikhael:** Late-serving restaurants that work as both kitchens and bars. A full mezze spread at 2 a.m. is normal here. Both are next to [Beirut – Downtown / Achrafieh](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator).

- **Hamra and Ras Beirut:** A more casual, student-friendly scene, with shawarma and falafel stands on Bliss Street that stay open long after the bars close. Our [Beirut – Hamra / Verdun / Ras Beirut](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) zone covers the whole area.
- **All-night manoushe bakeries:** Many near Hamra and Mar Mikhael bake flatbreads in wood-fired ovens, and they cost very little. They're a good last stop before you head home.
