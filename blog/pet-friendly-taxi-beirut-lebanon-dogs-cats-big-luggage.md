## Dog Friendly Cab in Lebanon: Tips for a Calm Ride

A few small preparations make the trip better for everyone.

### For dogs
- **Walk your dog first.** A short walk before the ride helps with nerves and with bathroom emergencies.
- **Use a leash, a harness or a crate.** Large, excited dogs are safer secured. Tell us the size so we can send the right vehicle.
- **Skip a big meal beforehand.** Car sickness is common, especially on winding roads like the Dhour el Choueir climb.
- **Bring a towel or blanket.** It keeps the seat clean and gives your dog a familiar scent.
- **Bring water.** Especially in summer, when Beirut's heat makes air conditioning important.

### For cats
- **Always use a carrier.** A hard-sided carrier that closes securely is the safest option. A loose cat in a moving car is a danger to itself and the driver.
- **Cover the carrier with a light cloth.** Less visual stimulation often means less stress.
- **Put a familiar blanket inside.** The scent of home helps.
- **Keep the carrier flat on the seat or on your lap.** Don't leave it on the floor, where it can slide.

### Our air-conditioned cars
Our vehicles are clean and air-conditioned. That matters in Lebanon, where a closed car in August can become dangerous for an animal within minutes. **Never leave a pet alone in a parked car.** If your appointment will be long, tell us and we will plan the waiting time with you.

## Traveling Through Beirut Airport With a Pet

Flying with a dog or cat means a long list of paperwork. Based on current published guidance, Lebanon asks for a rabies vaccination, a microchip, and a veterinary health certificate from the country of origin. An import permit from the Ministry of Agriculture is also commonly required. **Rules change, so confirm the latest requirements with your airline and the Lebanese Ministry of Agriculture before you travel.**

What we can control is the ground part of the journey:

- **Pickup or drop-off at [Beirut Airport (BEY)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator).**
- **Flight delay tracking.** If your flight is late, your driver is still there. You won't have an anxious pet waiting at arrivals while you look for a car.
- **Enough space for the crate.** Airline-approved travel crates are large. Tell us the dimensions and we will match the vehicle.
- **A fixed fare from the start.** There are no surprises after a long flight.
