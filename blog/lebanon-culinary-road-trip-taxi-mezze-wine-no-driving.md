## Route 1: The Bekaa Valley Winery Day Trip (Ksara, Kefraya and Zahle Mezze)

This is the classic **Bekaa valley winery day trip**, and the best choice if you want Lebanon's most storied estates in one day.

### The drive
From [Beirut – Downtown / Achrafieh](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) or [Beirut – Hamra / Verdun / Ras Beirut](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator), we take the Beirut–Damascus highway up and over Dahr el Baidar, then drop into the valley toward [Zahle](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). Allow roughly 1 to 1.5 hours each way, depending on traffic and weather.

### Stop 1: Chateau Ksara (a private tour of the Ksara winery)
Chateau Ksara is Lebanon's oldest winery, founded in 1857. It is famous for its roughly 2 km of Roman-era cave cellars, which keep a steady cool temperature. A **private tour of the Ksara winery** usually includes the caves, a look at production, and a tasting. This is the best first stop of the day: your palate is fresh and the cellar is cool.

### Stop 2: Lunch in Zahle
Zahle is known as the "Bride of the Bekaa" and has a strong food culture. The riverside **Berdawni** valley is lined with restaurants where the mezze is generous and the arak is part of the ritual. Expect a table of:

- Hummus, moutabbal and fresh tabbouleh
- Kibbeh nayyeh and grilled meats
- Fresh bread from the saj, local cheeses, and mountain herbs
- Arak with ice and water, if you want it
- Booza (Lebanese ice cream) or sweets afterwards

Berdawni gets busy at weekends, so tell us your preferred timing and we will plan around it. Having a driver means you can enjoy the arak without worrying about the road.

### Stop 3: Chateau Kefraya
After lunch we continue to the West Bekaa for **Chateau Kefraya**, one of the valley's best-known estates, set on a large vineyard with views toward the mountains. A late-afternoon tasting here is a good finish. The drive back to Beirut is yours to rest on.

If you want to add a third estate, such as Massaya, Chateau St. Thomas or Domaine Wardy, tell us when you book and we will plan the timing realistically.

**Best for:** first-time visitors, couples, small groups, and anyone who wants the classic Bekaa highlights.

## Route 2: Mount Lebanon Mountain Mezze and Batroun Wineries

Not every wine day has to go inland. This route stays on the hillsides, away from the beach scene, and focuses on vines, terraces and a long lunch.

### Batroun wineries with a private driver
The hills behind Batroun, around Basbina, are home to **IXSIR**, a winery that welcomes visitors with tours and a restaurant serving cuisine inspired by Lebanese terroirs, paired with its own wines. Ixsir is open Tuesday to Sunday and is closed on Mondays, and guided tours should be requested in advance. Please confirm current hours and book your slot directly with the winery. We can help with the logistics.

A **Batroun wineries private driver** handles the Autostrade north from [Jounieh / Kaslik / Zouk](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) and [Byblos (Jbeil)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator), then the climb into the hills where the signposted wine route begins. These small roads are easy to miss and awkward to drive after tasting.

### Mountain mezze lunch
Mount Lebanon is where the traditional "mezze with a view" lunch comes from. Terraced restaurants in the mountain villages serve seasonal dishes, mountain salads, labneh, grilled halloumi, kibbeh and arak. If you start from [Faraya / Kfardebian](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator), we can also build a cooler-weather route through the mountain villages.

**Best for:** travelers who prefer a slower, scenic day, and anyone coming from the Kesrouan or Metn side.
