# Beirut Airport Taxi: Fixed Fares Are Here. Here's How to Book a Price You Can Trust

For years, the first thing many travelers did after landing in Lebanon was negotiate a taxi fare. That is changing. Lebanon's Public Works Ministry has introduced a regulated fare schedule for taxis leaving Beirut's Rafic Hariri International Airport. Passengers can check it by scanning a QR code on the airport screens and on leaflets handed out at passport control.

We welcome this. Fixed pricing has always been our standard at Andrews Taxi. Here is what it means for your arrival, and how to plan the rest of your trip.

## Why a fixed fare matters

You land tired. You have luggage. You may not know the roads, the exchange rate, or what a fair price looks like. Haggling at that point is stressful, and it is the wrong way to begin a trip.

A fixed fare gives you three things:

- **No surprises.** You know the price before you get in the car.
- **No negotiating.** You don't compare quotes or argue at the curb.
- **Easier budgeting.** You can plan your trip cost in advance.

## Autumn day trip: a taxi to Byblos and Batroun

October and November are good months for exploring the coast. The summer crowds have thinned and the temperatures are comfortable. A **Lebanon private driver** makes the most of the day, because you stop where you like and don't depend on a bus timetable.

**Byblos (Jbeil).** One of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. Walk the Phoenician and Roman ruins, the Crusader castle, the old souk, and the harbor. A [taxi to Byblos](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) is a short drive from Beirut or the airport.

**Batroun.** A little further north, [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) has an old town with a Phoenician sea wall, historic churches, and the Nabu Museum. There are also local breweries, nearby vineyards, and seafood by the water.

**A simple itinerary:**

- Morning: pick-up from the airport or your hotel, then head to Byblos.
- Midday: explore the old city and have lunch by the port.
- Afternoon: continue to Batroun for the old town, a coffee, or a winery visit.
- Evening: return to Beirut, or stay for dinner. Your driver can wait, or you can book the return trip.

You'll have no parking to find and no driving after dinner.
