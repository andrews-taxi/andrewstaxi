# Taxi to Byblos and Batroun: The Perfect October Day Trip from Beirut

October is one of the best months to explore Lebanon. The heat has eased, the light over the Mediterranean is softer, and the coastal road north of Beirut is a pleasure to drive. If you have one free day, the best plan is a **taxi to Byblos** followed by an afternoon in Batroun.

## A suggested route

### 1. Start in Beirut
We can pick you up from your hotel or home in [Beirut – Downtown / Achrafieh](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) or [Beirut – Hamra / Verdun / Ras Beirut](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). Many visitors book a Beirut airport transfer straight to Byblos so they can begin the trip on arrival day.

### 2. Byblos (Jbeil), the morning stop
Byblos is often described as one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. Plan to see:
- The archaeological site and the Crusader castle
- The Phoenician harbour
- The old souk
- The medieval St. John-Marc Cathedral

Byblos is walkable, so your driver can drop you at the entrance and meet you when you finish. Book your [taxi to Byblos (Jbeil)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) ahead of time for a smooth start.

### 3. Batroun, the afternoon and evening stop
Continue up the coast to [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). Highlights include:
- The old town and its Phoenician sea wall
- The Our Lady of the Sea church
- Local lemonade and fresh seafood by the water
- Craft beer and wineries in the surrounding hills

This is also where the day turns into an evening. Enjoy dinner or a drink, and **order your taxi back** when you are ready. You never need to worry about driving after a glass of wine.
