## Why Beirut Professionals Are Switching to a Daily Work Driver

A daily **work commute taxi in Lebanon** is no longer a luxury for executives only. Office employees, bankers, lawyers, doctors, NGO staff, engineers, and teachers use dedicated drivers. Here is why.

### 1. Traffic stress is part of the job now
Beirut's main arteries clog at predictable times:
- **The northern Autostrade**, from Jounieh and Kaslik through Dbayeh, Antelias and Jal el Dib. Mornings toward Beirut and afternoons back north are the heaviest.
- **The Hazmieh – Sin el Fil – Charles Helou corridor** toward Achrafieh and Downtown.
- **The Ring and the Downtown approaches**, when events, roadworks or security closures hit.
- **The coastal and airport roads** from Chiyah, Bourj Brajneh and Ouzai toward Hamra and Verdun.

A driver who has done these roads for decades knows which shortcut to take on a bad morning. He also knows when to leave the Autostrade and how to enter Downtown by the quickest route. A navigation app can't match that judgment.

When you pre-book a monthly package, you agree the rate in advance and stop tracking pump prices.

### 3. Parking is a daily headache
Searching for a spot around Downtown, Achrafieh, Hamra or Verdun costs time. Paying for it costs money. A door-to-door drop-off removes both.

### 4. Your car stays in good shape
Daily stop-and-go driving wears out brakes, clutches, tires and suspension. It also adds up in oil changes, insurance, mechanic visits and depreciation. With a taxi package, none of that is on you.

## Daily Office Taxi vs. Driving Your Own Car: A Simple Cost Check

Every commute is different, so we won't pretend there's one price for everyone. Here is an **illustrative estimate** you can adapt to your own situation:

- A round trip of about 40 km per day, 22 working days a month, is roughly **880 km**.
- In city stop-and-go traffic, a typical car may use about 10–12 liters per 100 km. That is roughly **90–105 liters a month**.
- At about US$1.19 per liter, that is around **US$105–125 per month in fuel alone**.

Now add the costs most people forget:
- Daily or monthly parking fees
- Maintenance, tires and oil
- Insurance and annual registration
- Depreciation, plus unexpected repairs
- The value of your time, up to an hour or more lost to traffic and parking

Once you total all of that, a fixed monthly ride is often **competitive with, or cheaper than, driving yourself**. It also gives you your time and peace of mind back.
