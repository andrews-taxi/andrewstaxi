## Why Wedding Guest Transportation in Lebanon Needs a Plan

Lebanon's most popular wedding venues are scenic, which also means they are remote. That creates problems you won't have at a hotel ballroom in the city:

- **Mountain venues have narrow, winding roads.** Roads in the Chouf, the Metn heights and the Kesrouan hills have tight bends. Fog and rain can make them hard to drive, especially for someone who doesn't know them.
- **Parking is limited.** Many garden and estate venues have small car parks. Dozens of private cars create a bottleneck at arrival and a worse one at departure.
- **Late-night rides are hard to find.** Ride-hailing apps are unreliable when you are far from the city at 1 a.m. A driver may not accept the trip, or may cancel when he sees the destination.
- **Alcohol and fatigue are a real risk.** A celebration is no time for anyone to decide whether they are "fine to drive."
- **Guests are spread across the country.** Some stay in Beirut, some in Jounieh, and others fly in and need transfers from the airport.

A pre-arranged shuttle or private driver removes all of these worries. The family can enjoy the night without wondering how everyone will get home.

## Wedding Shuttle Van Lebanon: Which Vehicle Fits Your Guest List?

Matching the vehicle to the group makes the transfers smoother and usually saves money.

### Private car (sedan): the couple, parents and VIPs
- Best for the bride and groom, close family, elderly relatives and special guests.
- Pickup from the hotel, the salon or the family home, and a quiet, comfortable ride.
- Good for the "arrival moment" and for a calm exit after the last dance.

### Minivan: families and friend groups
- Ideal for groups travelling from the same area, such as a cousins' van from Achrafieh or a friends' van from Hamra.
- Everyone arrives and leaves together, with no one hunting for a taxi at midnight.
- It costs much less per person than separate cars.

### Multiple vehicles on a fixed schedule: large weddings
- Send a rolling series of vans to different pickup zones before the ceremony.
- Run staggered return trips, for example a first wave at 11:30 p.m. for families with children and older guests, and a late wave after the cake and final dances.
- Keep one car on standby at the venue for emergencies or early departures.

## Mountain Wedding Venue Taxi: What to Expect in Chouf, Broummana and the Metn

Mountain weddings are beautiful, and the logistics deserve respect. If you are planning a mountain event, keep these points in mind.

- **Allow extra travel time.** The Autostrade and the mountain roads behind it can move slowly, especially on weekend evenings and during rain. Leave early so that guests arrive before the ceremony, not during it.
- **Expect cooler weather and fog.** Evenings in the mountains get cold quickly in autumn and winter. A driver with experience on these roads is a significant safety advantage.
- **Plan for narrow access roads.** Some venues have a steep or narrow final approach. Experienced drivers know where larger vans can turn and wait safely.
- **Agree a pickup point.** Tell guests exactly where to meet their van after the party, such as the main gate, the reception door or a marked area. This avoids a confusing search in the dark.
- **Share the schedule.** Give us the ceremony time, the expected end time and any special requests. We will plan the pickups and the return trips around it.
