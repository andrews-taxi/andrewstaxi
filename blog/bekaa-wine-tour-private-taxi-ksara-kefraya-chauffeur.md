## The Wineries: What to Visit on a Bekaa Valley Wine Tour

### Chateau Ksara: The Classic First Stop (Near Zahle)

For a **private tour of the Ksara winery**, this is the natural starting point. Ksara is the oldest and best-known winery in Lebanon, and it sits just outside [Zahle](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). Its main draw is a network of long underground limestone tunnels used as natural cellars. They're cool all year and make an atmospheric setting for a tasting.

**Tip:** Ksara is easy to reach from the main road, so it works well as the first stop on the way into the valley. Book a guided cellar visit ahead if you want the full story.

### Chateau Kefraya: West Bekaa Views and a Full Tasting

Chateau Kefraya sits in the West Bekaa, on a different side of the valley from Ksara. The estate is known for its hillside vineyards and a polished visitor experience. Pairing **Chateau Ksara and Kefraya** in one day is the most popular plan we get. It gives you two styles and two landscapes, and it needs a driver who knows the connecting roads.

That's why searches for a **Chateau Ksara Kefraya driver** usually end in a private car. Public transport doesn't connect the two, and renting a car puts the driving on you.

### Massaya: Bekaa and Mount Lebanon

Massaya has two bases: its Bekaa estate and the Faqra winery in Mount Lebanon, where vines grow at high altitude. Visits and meals are by reservation, and Faqra's restaurant service has been listed for Friday to Sunday. Confirm directly before you go.

### Ixsir: The Mount Lebanon and Batroun Connection

Ixsir produces wines from vineyards across Lebanon, and its winery near Batroun welcomes visitors Tuesday to Sunday. It offers a free self-guided tour, a complimentary tasting of two wines, and a bookable guided tour. Guided tours need advance notice, so request them well ahead of your trip.

If you're looking for a **Batroun wineries private driver**, Ixsir is the one to build around. It's a winery-and-lunch day, not a beach day. Combine it with a second Batroun-area winery and a long lunch, then return along the Autostrade at your own pace.

> **Always confirm opening hours and tasting reservations directly with each winery.** Hours change by season and by day of the week. Send us your plan on WhatsApp and we'll sequence the stops around them.

## Authentic Lebanese Food Tour With a Private Driver

Wine is half the day. The other half is lunch. For an **authentic Lebanese food tour private driver** experience, we build in a proper stop:

- **Zahle's riverside restaurants (Berdawni).** This is classic Bekaa dining, with mezze, grilled meats and arak by the water. Arak and wine at lunch is exactly why you hired a driver.
- **Mezze that matches the region.** Expect tabbouleh, hummus, kibbeh, labneh, grilled halloumi and fresh bread. Ask for the seasonal dishes.
- **Bekaa produce stops.** On the road you'll pass roadside stands selling nuts, dried fruit, honey and cheese. A driver who knows the good ones saves you time.
- **Baalbek add-on.** If you have a full day and want something different, some guests pair wine with the Roman ruins at [Baalbek](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). It makes for a long day, so tell us when booking.

This is what makes a **Lebanon culinary road trip taxi** different from a standard transfer. We plan around meals and tastings, not just the route.
