# Beirut After Dark: An Autumn Night-Out Guide (and How to Get Home Safely)

October is a good month to go out in Beirut. The evenings are cooler, the terraces are still open, and the city stays busy late into the night. Locals and visitors are out in the bars, restaurants and live-music venues. The harder part of a night out is usually the ride, not the plan. Parking is tight and you may not want to drive after a drink. Andrews Taxi covers that part: **fixed prices, professional drivers, and service 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.**

## Where to Go: Beirut's Best Nightlife Areas

### Mar Mikhael and Gemmayzeh
These two streets are the centre of Beirut's bar and restaurant scene. Small pubs, cocktail bars, live music and late dinners sit close together. Venues change with the seasons, so ask around once you arrive. If you are staying in [Beirut – Downtown / Achrafieh](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator), you are only a short ride away. Traffic and parking are the usual problems here, and a taxi avoids both.

### Hamra and Ras Beirut
Hamra is lively and central, with cafés, bars and some of the city's best late-night food. If you are heading to [Beirut – Hamra / Verdun / Ras Beirut](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator), book your return ride ahead of time so you are not waiting on the street at closing time.

### Badaro, Sodeco and the Clubs
Badaro has a relaxed bar scene. Sodeco and the surrounding districts have the nightclubs. If your group is split between several hotels or neighbourhoods, ask us for more than one car.

### Beyond Beirut: Jounieh, Kaslik and Zouk
The coast north of the capital has its own nightlife. A night out in [Jounieh / Kaslik / Zouk](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) is easy to reach from Beirut. You can also go the other way and head home to Beirut afterwards, with a driver who knows the roads.

## Late-Night Food: The Manoushe Tradition

Late-night eating is part of going out in Beirut. After the bars, many people go for a hot manoushe, flatbread with za'atar, cheese or meat. Bakeries in Hamra and Mar Mikhael keep their ovens going late. Tell your driver where you would like to stop and we will take you there.
