# Baalbek and Zahle Day Trip from Beirut: Why a Lebanon Private Driver Beats the Tour Bus

Autumn is one of the best times to see Lebanon. The heat has eased, the light is softer, and the Bekaa Valley is at its most scenic. If you are in Beirut this weekend, a day in the Bekaa is an easy choice. It combines Roman temples, a long Lebanese lunch and a winery visit.

The question is how to get there. Andrews Taxi covers this route every week, and here is how we suggest doing it.

## Suggested itinerary: Beirut → Zahle → Baalbek → Beirut

### 1. Early start from Beirut
Leave Beirut around 8:00 AM to beat the traffic on the mountain road. Your driver knows the best timing and the current road conditions, so you don't have to work them out yourself.

### 2. Zahle: brunch and river views
Our first stop is [Zahle](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator), known as the "Bride of the Bekaa." Walk along the Berdawni river and have a traditional Lebanese brunch. Expect fresh manakish, labneh, grilled meats and mezze. Your driver can recommend a place that suits your group.

### 3. Chateau Ksara or another Bekaa winery
The Bekaa is Lebanon's main wine region. Ksara is known for its historic Roman-era caves. A tasting is much easier when you aren't driving. This is a good reason to hire a driver in the first place.

### 4. Baalbek: the main stop
Next is [Baalbek](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator). It has some of the best-preserved Roman ruins in the world, including the Temple of Bacchus and the Temple of Jupiter. Allow at least two hours here. Wear comfortable shoes, and bring water and a light jacket, because autumn afternoons in the valley can turn cool quickly.

### 5. Return to Beirut
Your driver takes you back to your door, or to [Beirut Airport (BEY)](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) if you are heading out the same night.

## Tips for your Bekaa day trip

1. **Book the day before** so your driver and car are confirmed. Last-minute bookings are possible too, 24/7.
2. **Carry some cash** for entrance tickets, snacks and tips. Tipping isn't compulsory, but it is always appreciated.
3. **Dress in layers.** Mornings and evenings in the Bekaa are cooler than in Beirut.
4. **Bring a charged phone or camera.** The ruins are very photogenic, especially in late-afternoon light.
