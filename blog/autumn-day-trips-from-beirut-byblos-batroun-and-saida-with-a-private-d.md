# Autumn Day Trips from Beirut: Byblos, Batroun and Saida with a Private Driver

Late October brings cooler days and softer light to the Lebanese coast. The summer crowds have gone. It is a good time to walk old souks, climb castle walls and eat seafood without the heat.

You don't need to rent a car or work out the roads yourself. A private driver takes you from door to door at a price agreed before the ride. Below are three coastal day trips, and some tips for getting to and from the airport.

## Day Trip 1: Byblos (Jbeil), a Port Town Thousands of Years Old

Byblos is one of the oldest continuously inhabited towns in the world. It is a short drive north of Beirut. You can see the Crusader castle, the Roman remains, the old harbour and the stone alleys of the souk.

**Plan your day:**
1. Leave Beirut in the morning to avoid traffic.
2. Walk the archaeological site and the old souk.
3. Have a long lunch by the harbour.
4. Stay for the sunset, then ride back.

## Day Trip 2: Batroun, Seafood, Lemonade and Old Stone Streets

Batroun is a little further up the coast. It has a small old town, a sea-facing promenade, local lemonade and plenty of good places to eat. Many visitors combine Byblos and Batroun in one longer day.

A **Lebanon private driver** is useful for this route. You can stop where you like, stay as long as you want, and not worry about parking or the drive home after dinner. Check your fare for [Batroun](https://www.andrewstaxi.com/#fare-estimator) before you go.

## Day Trip 3: Saida (Sidon), Sea Castle and Old Market

South of Beirut, Saida has a castle on the water and a covered old market. The soap museum and the khans are also worth a visit. It is a good half-day or full-day outing for anyone who wants history and local food.
