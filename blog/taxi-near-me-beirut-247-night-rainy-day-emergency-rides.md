## Rainy-Day Taxi in Beirut: Why Bad Weather Changes Everything

Anyone who has driven in Lebanon in November knows what a downpour does. Local news outlets report the same pattern after heavy rain: flooded roads, stalled traffic and drivers stuck for hours.

Based on these reports and our own drivers' experience, the usual trouble spots in Greater Beirut are:

- **Nahr el Mot and the Dbayeh highway toward Antelias.** Backups happen here even in normal weather, and rain makes them worse.
- **Dora and the Nahr el Kalb stretch of the coastal highway.** Standing water and slow-moving lanes are common.
- **The airport road and its underpasses.** Flooding has happened here before, so allow extra time for every airport run in a storm.
- **Karantina and Bourj Hammoud.** Low-lying areas that collect water quickly.
- **Tunnels and underpasses in general.** Water can build up fast, and these are the places where cars get stuck.
