## Why Beirut's Nightlife Strips and Cars Don't Mix

Beirut's nightlife is compact, lively, and crowded.

- **Mar Mikhael** is centred on Armenia Street, the densest bar corridor in the city. It has converted warehouses, cocktail rooms, and live music.
- **Gemmayze** is on Gouraud Street. It has older Ottoman- and Mandate-era buildings and a bar-and-restaurant crowd that stays out late.
- **Badaro** is a more residential strip near the National Museum. It has café-bars, wide tree-lined streets, and a relaxed late-evening crowd.
- **Monnot** is still known for its bars and clubs, even though the crowd has spread to other areas over the years.

These streets are narrow, and they're busiest on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday nights. That's the same time parking is hardest to find.
